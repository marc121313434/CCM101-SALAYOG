Working through this mission gave me a deeper appreciation of the three major cloud platforms: AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform. Of the three, the one that impressed me the most was **Amazon Web Services (AWS)**. Its massive global infrastructure and wide range of services make it feel like the most versatile option, capable of supporting everything from small projects to enterprise-scale systems. The maturity of AWS and the trust it has earned across industries really stood out.  

At the same time, I noticed that AWS, Azure, and GCP share many similarities. All of them provide the same foundational building blocks: virtual machines, object storage, networking, identity management, and managed Kubernetes. They also emphasize scalability, security, and reliability. In practice, this means that while each platform has its own strengths, they all aim to solve the same core problems for businesses moving to the cloud.  

One of the biggest lessons I learned is how much **business requirements influence cloud selection**. A university that already uses Microsoft tools will naturally benefit from Azure’s seamless integration. A startup with limited funds might lean toward GCP for its pricing and developer-friendly ecosystem. Meanwhile, a global e-commerce company would likely choose AWS for its reach and reliability. Matching the right platform to the right need is the key to successful cloud adoption.  

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me think critically about these choices. It wasn’t just about listing services, but about analyzing scenarios and making recommendations that actually fit. This sharpened my ability to connect technical solutions with real-world business goals.  

Finally, my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio has grown into something I’m proud of. By documenting comparisons, recommendations, decision matrices, and reflections, I now have a structured showcase of my cloud knowledge. It demonstrates both technical understanding and consulting skills, which I know will be valuable for my academic work and future career in IT.
